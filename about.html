About Sable

We know what a missed call costs

Sable was built for the trades — the plumbers, electricians, and HVAC pros who lose real money every time the phone rings and no one can pick up.

We started Sable after watching a plumber friend lose a $1,400 emergency job to voicemail at 11pm — the caller simply dialed the next name on the list. It happens to trades every single day.

So we built the one thing that actually moves the needle: an assistant that answers every call and quotes the job accurately on the spot. Quoting is our craft. Everything else — dispatch, the live map, the customer records, the review bot — exists to turn that answered call into booked, paid, five-star work.

No call center. No script that frustrates your customers. Just a front office that never sleeps, priced per truck instead of per headache.
